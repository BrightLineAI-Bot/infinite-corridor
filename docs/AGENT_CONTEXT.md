## Systems

- **Underlands (dungeon generator 5, release 96)** — `src/underlands.ts`. Six layout grammars (spine, hub, ring, cavern, crypt, descent); about 45% of crossings get a themed environment (Sunless Chasm, Lava Bog, Underground River, Frozen Grotto, Bone Crypt, Fungal Hollow) with elevation, lethal terrain and weather. Applies only to histories without a pinned `generatorVersion`; v2/3/4 histories keep their generators.
- **Relay network (release 96)** — `src/relay-network.ts`. About 35% of never-entered ordinary crossings pin a relay node near the guardian. After the guardian falls the player restores it (restorative draught) or severs it (two lumen dust); each answer journals the next draft fragment. At 5 answers the network records `save.relayNetwork.coordinate` off the charted map. Nothing is generated at the coordinate.
- **Landscape (releases 94, 97, 101)** — `src/landscape.ts`, `src/landscape-render.ts`. Noise-driven biomes and elevation 0–5 on overworld sections (adoption pinned per atlas entry via `landscapeVersion`). Level marks and traversal cues show heights; `ensureReturnable` removes trap pockets; release 101 adds walkable ramps between one-level steps (`addRamps`). Falls of 4+ levels, pits/chasms, lava, rivers and deep ocean water kill; shallows are walkable.
- **Monster pack and grotesques (releases 92, 96)** — `src/monster-pack.ts`. Creatures are compositions of body, movement mode, brain, attack modules and stats. Grotesques (Weeping Saint, Amalgam, Hanged Choir, Gnawing Maw; Sunderjaw) are dungeon-only.
- **Glyphs & Resonances (release 98)** — `src/glyphs.ts`. Ten glyphs socketed into primary, tool and spell hosts; eight two-glyph Resonances. Drops, salvage dust, crafting.
- **Creature Echoes (release 99)** — `src/echoes.ts`. One Echo per pack creature, three notches; Echoes add triggered moves on dodge/land/kill/hurt/spell/shot.
- **Aperture Constellation (release 100)** — `src/constellation.ts`. Five branches (Form, Motion, Veil, Force, Rift) with ranks, a modifier and two capstones (exclusive until release 109; a mastered branch may now take both, the second in `caps2`); one point per 6 Aperture. Respec only at shrines (Attune) for lumen dust.
- **Awakenings (release 100)** — at Sela's forge, a max-rank weapon holding a Resonance can be Awakened for 6 lumen dust (crescent every third swing; tools fan shots into three).
- **Proving Ground** — `proving-ground/` plus `src/proving-ground*.ts`. Isolated scratch-state labs: ordinary/deep/threefold dungeons, Underlands & Relay, Landscape, Glyph Forge, combat arena, hunts/domains, inventory, magic/Aperture, performance, save/death/recovery, scenes, records, shelters, with scenarios such as Sandbox (every glyph), Echoes (all owned), Constellation + Awakening, and Relay node. Never touches production journeys.

## Tim's standing decisions

- Dungeon and corridor lore stays ambiguous: the dungeons are real places beneath the corridor, and also possibly elsewhere — an infinity of infinities. Do not resolve it.
- The relay story fragments (`FRAGMENTS`) are drafts Tim will rewrite. The relay coordinate leads to the Far Array (release 138, Tim's 2026-10-16 decision): the Array Mouth, runs whose guardians lock pieces of the Far Gate, the Listener behind each whole Gate, ascending tiers with boons. Keep its lore open; the Listener's lines and fragments are drafts Tim may rewrite.
- Falls of 4+ levels, pits and deep water are lethal on purpose. Release 101 removed the edge guard; do not add it back.
- Respec happens only at shrines, for lumen dust.
- Death (2026-10-16): only raw materials gathered in the world for building or survival are lost on death — wood today (`loseWood`), and stone or anything similar added later must follow the same rule. Spheres, cinder iron, lumen dust, glyphs, equipment, marks and XP are never lost.
- Release numbers (updated 2026-10-16, after release 149): two cloud sessions build in parallel. The towns/reciters/dungeons session takes even release numbers. The diagnostics/remediation session takes odd ones (the water/boats session, which held odd numbers, finished at release 124). If main has passed your number, jump to your next number of your own parity; never take the other parity. Before each merge, fetch main, merge it in, re-bump if needed, and message the other session the number you are taking.
- Working model (Tim, 2026-11-05; supersedes the parallel-session parity rule above): one session builds, coordinating the specialist agents in `.claude/agents/` (world-builder, world-beasts, dungeons, monsters, player-actions, spells-and-builds, travel-and-teleport, ui-and-hud, story-and-lore, saves-and-persistence, performance, combat-balance, qa-fuzzer, release-reviewer). Independent tasks go to agents in parallel worktrees; the session integrates their branches, takes one release number per PR (`main`'s release + 1), and runs the release-reviewer before every publish. Do not split work across several cloud sessions.
- The Listener cycle (Tim, 2026-11-05): one Mouth per Gate, its coordinates fixed until that Gate is unlocked. Every 5 relays (severed or left open) a Listener returns to that Mouth, with a notice to go back to it. Each Listener kill locks a Gate piece, and so does each Gate Revenant horde dungeon; 5 pieces unlock the Gate. A Mouth's town rises on the first Listener kill there. Transit Coordinates come once per Gate, on its unlock, and lead to the next Mouth; the Journal's Far Array tab shows the pieces and the current coordinate, or that there is none yet.
- The Doom Chaser lives only in the Stillworks (Tim, 2026-11-05). Great trees keep plenty of tall spires (2026-11-05). Spell (Focus) damage is left as is for Tim to test before any caster tuning (2026-11-05).
- Story density (Tim, 2026-11-05): "When we're adding new stories, the Corridor is going to be huge, massive. I don't really want to increase story density, but my goal is to get out to 1000 and then beyond, so we should have story density going out that far. I don't want the game to feel overly filled with these stories, but I don't want it to be sparse either. The density it has now is pretty good — going through the world you'll see a bell or a link or something like that. I don't want to increase it. Find a way to merge these stories into the whole Corridor without making it too many stories." Built as the story budget (src/threads.ts `STORY_BUDGET`, docs/narrative-bible.md section 6 item 6): one fixed thread-piece chance per crossing (0.13) from 3 sections out to 1000 and beyond, in crossings first entered from then on; new threads join the shared roll (a new `shared` version key), never a new wave, so they split slots rather than add them. Crossings entered before keep what they offered.
- Story density, later (Tim, 2026-11-05/06): trimmed to 0.12 (story version 2); the far realms past 1000 get their own threads, more of them is welcome ("the world is huge and I intended it to be massive") and they should be the main stories out there (versions 3 and 4: five far threads at weight 3; from v4 the first-ten slots past 1000 go to the shared roll). Every change is a new story version for crossings first pinned after it.
- Echoes add moves; they never replace dodge or jump. The 5 stat points stay.
- Publishing (2026-10-18; replaces the PC watcher and Discord card): CI, previews and production publishing run on GitHub Actions. Every merge to main is previewed automatically at `previews/r<N>-<commit12>/`. Production publishes only after a Fable review of everything since the live release and Tim saying "publish" in the session; the session then runs the Publish workflow. No external approval gate. The procedure is in `AGENTS.md` (Publishing) and `docs/github-actions-release.md`.
- Content stages (2026-10-29; not the in-game creature stages S1/S2 that the Listener opens): Stage 1 is the base game, the first creatures, monsters and world beasts. Stage 2 is everything up to now: the passives and secondary passives, the extra weapons and brawler gear, the newer bosses. Stage 3 begins with vertical traversal: climbable giant trees, skyways and floating platforms (jumped, or boosted across in the robot) that lead to sky areas and carry you somewhere new, and stranger biomes. Tim's open thoughts for it: further map layers (the buoys' ocean, the skyways, perhaps an underground of canyons) and harder heavenly challenges (several world bosses at once).
