## Explicit implementation choices and outstanding design acceptance

1. **S4 resolves the earlier protected-region exception.** Every valid in-bounds terrain cell touched by the Supernova disk is consumed, including progression gates, shortcuts, thin building walls, occupied floor and map-edge tiles. There is no protected-gate/disconnected-room refusal. Physical route bypass is intentional. Quest/object states, objectives, rewards, discovery, actors and items are not completed, deleted or moved merely because the floor changes. Deep-progress refresh preserves existing scars rather than rebuilding a physical gate or repainting an opened shortcut. Surviving walls outside the footprint still stop movement and particles.
2. **S5 exact circle versus tile collision.** The persisted continuous disk matches the native Supernova center `(effect.x + .5, effect.y + .62)` and radius. Whole collision tiles intersecting that disk become harmless floor because the existing game has tile-based collision, not partial-cell terrain. Only the white/red material is clipped to the true circular union; leftover corners of an intersected boundary tile are ordinary safe floor, never misleading surviving wall artwork. Tangent-only/out-of-bounds cells are not consumed. This sub-tile quantization is an explicit engineering distinction, not a claim that collision acquired arbitrary circular polygons.
3. Dungeon and overworld scar records remain additive at save schema 14: `save.voidScars = {schema:1, areas:{[exactAreaStateId]:{width,height,elevation:0,cells:['x,y',…], elevations?, slopes?, circles?, legacyCells?}}}`. Sparse per-cell heights/slopes preserve safe floor topography; exact circles retain their center/radius. Each circle also retains its cast-center `visualLift`. The static material uses that constant projection plane to remain a true screen-space circle matching the native blast even across uneven floor heights; underlying physical floor heights stay unchanged. This is an explicit rendering approximation, not a terrain flattening operation. Unknown fields and earlier cell-only records survive. Dungeon level suffixes and overworld section/generation keys isolate scars; base generation stays pinned and unchanged. Exact repeats and contained circles can be deduplicated, but history is never evicted by a fixed area count. Runtime casts, replay and beast bookkeeping inspect only the active area record; the renderer receives only the current map’s circles and caches their geometry. Fresh-save/migration and whole-journey serialization necessarily process saved history. Distinct overlapping outlines in one section can still increase its render/cache rebuild work. Whole-journey storage necessarily grows and remains subject to the save backend's capacity/error handling; indefinite game persistence is not infinite physical storage.
4. **S4 requires both worlds.** Overworld revisits and full save reload replay scars after generated terrain, building and object state restoration, just like dungeon revisits. Earlier I-07 / visual-only-overworld behavior is superseded. Non-wall quest entities and loot remain; building artwork is clipped where physical wall terrain was consumed. No new interactive scar object or Atlas marker is created.
5. The Supernova field and blast origin stay at the cast location while Walker teleports around the current player. This is the declared I-10 choice. Dashes remain active when the player leaves the region, but not across unloaded scenes or owner death/retreat.
6. Wraiths commit one straight travel vector and make three transient slashes without curving or re-aiming. They are effects, not summoned enemy actors. No loot, durable army, or extra actor slots result.
7. A constellation rend has one activation; different rends can produce a wraith, beam or Walker teleport concurrently. Persistent non-constellation rends have a separate finite lifetime and emission limit.
8. **S6/S7 supersede the earlier retreat-rift fallback.** Ordinary patrol and combat obey ground geometry, and combat rifts remain separate. Retreat uses regular wall-respecting walking and one-level jumping toward the nearest reachable dungeon doorway by legal path distance. S7 explicitly permits the encounter to end at a physical inter-level doorway/stair passage, rather than simulating the entire trip outside. Eligible generated targets are `exit` and physical `deepTransition` objects; arbitrary internal room doors and magical `deepPortal` objects are not substitutes. Legacy single-level maps can use their represented entrance. A blocked route retries safely, without teleporting, phasing or prematurely disappearing. Vanishing happens only after reaching a real target. The earlier entrance-only retreat and disconnected fallback rift are historical behavior, not the accepted final design.
9. A Walker hunt is pinned to its first dungeon level, preventing a fresh-health copy on each floor. Unfinished retreat pauses while its source area/level is unloaded, preserves the same health/position/identity, and resumes on revisit; leaving does not falsely complete the escape. Crystal level behavior is unchanged. Reload cancels transient attacks/visuals under the existing pack runtime contract, while preserving health, cooldowns, hunt tries, precise legal position and persistent terrain. Pause with dt 0 freezes Walker simulation; actual `Game.setPaused` resume also preserves the new Echo warning and cooldown deadlines. Full attack-phase continuation across reload was not added.
10. Proposed Echo, not a dictated mechanic: **Rift Upheaval** makes dodging leave one delayed void-spike eruption under a nearby foe, using the established bounded Echo patch path. It has its own data table and new tests. Tim did not specify a Walker Echo.
11. Spawn rate, level/depth gates, damage, recovery, radii and lifetimes are initial balance proposals, not user-supplied numbers. `VOID`, `ATTACK_MODULES.void*`, `WORLD_BEASTS.voidWalker`, `WALKER_ROUTE`, `VOID_SCARS` and `VOID_ECHO` are the tuning sources. In particular: three hunt attempts; level 8/depth 5 ordinary-dungeon gate; 3–6 constellation portals; .48-second basic rend warning; 7-tile Supernova radius; 2–3 particle waves; six teleport-dash opportunities; .28-second dash warning; one-second trails. Field/rift/collision pressure needs player feedback.
12. All pack effects share the existing cap of 48. Walker emission refuses excess effects instead of evicting another creature's telegraph. Patrol plans cap at 625 nodes; long retreat searches retain their frontier across frames with at most 625 expansions per call. Landing connectivity is finite and cached with topology invalidation. On-device performance remains unverified.
13. **Scar-only safe elevation joins.** Existing safe floor heights/slopes are preserved. Consumed walls/pits inherit a deterministic nearest-safe-floor height. A scar can join floors farther apart than an ordinary one-level ramp; `safeVoidScarTransition` makes that newly connected edge traversable for player/Walker and suppresses new fall damage there without editing non-scar terrain. It does not bypass a surviving wall, pit, void or lethal floor. Accepted X-then-Y movement substeps split fall calculations at safe scar edges, so a diagonal entering and leaving a scar in one frame neither fabricates a lethal fall nor grants immunity to a later ordinary cliff. Ordinary non-scar cliffs retain their previous lethal fall rules. Reachability/returnability use the same rule so valid saved positions are not falsely repaired. The existing renderer still projects actual stored terrain elevations; this is not a new flight or wall-phasing ability.
14. **Fog and static material.** Active Supernova visuals draw over unexplored fog, but do not change the revealed set, show underlying ground or reveal enemies/objects. After the finite combat effects expire, undiscovered scar ground remains concealed. Normal exploration reveals a crisp white, nonanimated circle with sparse fine red fractures. No scar damage, interaction, map marker or self-expiry exists. S5's reduced crack density is an art choice subject to Tim's visual feedback, not a numeric requirement he specified.

15. **Loaded-section boundary.** Terrain mutation and the visible circle are clipped to the current loaded map bounds. An overworld blast does not generate or modify neighboring unloaded sections. Exact center/radius remains stored, but no cross-section blast propagation is claimed. New tests retain neighboring area records and verify clean new-journey history independently of an old scarred save.
