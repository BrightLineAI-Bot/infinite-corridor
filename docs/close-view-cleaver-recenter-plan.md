## 7b: reuse Target activation and the camera solver

Current `mountCloseViewControls.activateLock` calls only `closeViewLockToggle`; it already deduplicates pointer-release/click and preserves keyboard/AT activation. `closeViewRecentre` requests yaw from lastAim/facing; it does not remove shoulder or lateral rig offset. Lock tick currently supersedes that yaw request. Simply calling the two functions consecutively cannot satisfy centered rear framing.

Add `closeViewTargetActivate(g, now)` in close-view.ts as the single mounted-button command: reject paused/not-showing input; perform the existing lock toggle ONCE using the pre-recenter view for acquisition; then request centered rear even on no candidate or unlock. Keep swipe switching/death-rehop/LOS/range/visibility rules. Leave direct world-tap targeting semantics unchanged unless separately requested. Main and PG already share the mounted controls; change callers only if an actual duplicate activation is found. No Game.ts changes are needed for camera behavior.

Extend `closeViewRecentre(g,now,{centered:false}={})` compatibly. Target passes centered:true; ordinary crescent double-tap retains its existing contract. Keep one preallocated runtime `CAM.centeredRear` flag plus existing recenter goal/spring state; no saved camera schema. While centered:

- Resolve the rear heading from the same final body-facing precedence as rendering. Idle eligible lock uses the selected bearing and existing displayed turn; active accepted action, charge, dodge or hit retains its current body facing. Reuse/extract that facing resolution locally rather than writing lastAim, meleeStrike or pending-tool aim. Camera settles behind the resulting body, and ongoing lock may then follow the target. Exact target/body alignment is deferred during a committed action, not forced by corrupting its accepted geometry.
- Use the existing yaw/recenter rates and pause clock, settling to the exact requested heading within the existing tolerance; reset user pitch toward the preset as the existing recenter does. Explicit Target supersedes an old quick-turn/recenter request and drops pre-activation pending camera deltas; later manual orbit cancels centeredRear/recenter immediately and retains normal lock-swipe behavior. Do not reset held movement into a new direction mid-stroke: keep the existing latched inputYaw contract until neutral, then synchronize it. Reset the mode on map/camera reset or leaving Close View; retain it while stationary/moving/locked until manual orbit or reset, so shoulder does not silently return one frame later.
- Force shoulder and rigOffset (including their velocities/hysteresis) to zero and remove transverse follow lag while centered. Disable lateral clearance candidate selection in this mode. Keep longitudinal follow, existing boom shortening, ceiling/crane, near-plane/body framing and final wall/sight checks. Obstruction shortens the rear boom; it never teleports the player, moves geometry, switches to a lateral shoulder, or rotates to an unrelated heading to fake space.
- Paused/background state does not move camera or queue a hidden Target action. Preserve native button semantics, focus, 52px hit area, drag rejection and aria-pressed lock state; update accessible help to explain both recenter and lock. Normal/reduced-motion modes retain their existing rates/safety constraints and no added zoom flourish.

The unavoidable tradeoff is shorter/closer framing when directly behind is obstructed; the old lateral escape cannot simultaneously remain active and meet zero lateral offset. Preserve safety, allow immediate manual orbit, and judge tight-corner usability in hosted acceptance.
